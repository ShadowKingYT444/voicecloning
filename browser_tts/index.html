About this prototype. Nano decodes each complete passage before playback. The reader keeps at most two passages queued and saves temporary audio to browser storage. Save a WAV to keep it. Abandoned recordings are cleaned up when a new reading starts in browsers supporting Web Locks. The under-500-MiB target is unverified. Passage joins can change rhythm or add a pause. This quantized Nano export is a community conversion. It has not passed a broad speech-quality or browser-compatibility evaluation.

Research output. Watermark preservation in the community decoder is unverified. This app does not apply native Perth watermarking. Browser WAVs are not release-ready native equivalents.
